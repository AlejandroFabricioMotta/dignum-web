Our philosophy

Technology is a decision about values.

Words from the Founder

I had promised myself I would never start another company.

I built more than twenty ventures, mentored more than two hundred entrepreneurs, and for thirteen years I have been building the Global Mentors Network. Then I watched artificial intelligence enter companies with no plan: people afraid, and decades of knowledge quietly walking out the door with every retirement.

I understood that this is not a technical problem. It is a decision about values, and it falls to those who lead.

That is why I created Dignum. Many companies know how to integrate AI; Dignum does too. What sets us apart is what we do at the same time: we train technology natives and we reinvent traditional senior professionals. It is what a socially responsible leader must do.

I have captured this entire vision in the Manual “People and AI, with dignity,” which is the philosophical foundation of Dignum.

If you lead an organization and feel that AI cannot move forward at the expense of your people... here is your ally to make it happen.

Our vision

A world of work where people and AI agents coexist in a healthy way, and where no one loses their dignity because a technology arrived before a plan.

Our mission

  • Design mixed org charts together with the people of each organization, not over their heads.
  • Build AI agents with limits, values, and security.
  • Create custom wisdom agents that preserve the experience of each company's best people.

Eleven guiding principles

  1. A person's dignity comes first.
  2. No automation without a transition plan.
  3. Complement, don't compete.
  4. Values define what technology does.
  5. Human oversight, always.
  6. Wisdom is guided, not dictated.
  7. Experience belongs to those who lived it and to those who hire it.
  8. Security and confidentiality by design.
  9. Impact before profitability.
  10. Transparency and truth.
  11. A legacy that does not depend on a single person.
  12. People and AI, with dignity.

Close to the RGM, but independent

Dignum shares the beacon of the Global Mentors Network (RGM), but is an independent company. Dignum puts agents where work has become dehumanizing; the RGM humanizes professions and trades through mentoring, so that people complement AI and do not compete with it.

How we measure our impact

Not only with a balance sheet. We seek the economic balance to sustain ourselves, and we measure success by the voice of the people whose work changed: testimonials gathered by an independent third party, including criticism, and follow-up at 6 and 12 months.

What we do

Solutions and services for mixed organizations.

We support companies that want to bring in AI agents without losing their people or their knowledge. We work with time and care, a few organizations at a time.

01

Mixed org charts

Together with the company's people, we design which roles an AI agent takes on and which are humanized. Every change includes a transition plan: reassignment, retraining, or upskilling.

02

AI agents with limits

We build agents that know what they can do, what they cannot, and who they answer to. With human oversight, security, and confidentiality by design.

03

Wisdom agents

We create, tailored to each company, an agent that gathers its regulations, its data, and the reflected experience of its best people. It does not dictate: it guides. That way the judgment of those who retire remains available to those who arrive.

Solutions from technology partners

Besides our own developments, we represent solutions from tech entrepreneurs that already work in real companies. We choose them for their quality and for their consistency with our principles, and we integrate them into the design of each organization.

Concrete solutions

We automate the repetitive so your people can do what matters.

Data and customers

Well-known customers

We organize, clean, and enrich your customer and contact databases, and tag them by interests and profile. That way your teams know who to talk to and about what.

One record per person

We detect duplicates and entry errors and sync your systems (web, CRM, forms, billing). Information stops being scattered.

Memory kept safe

We design scheduled, verified backups stored outside the original platform. Your critical information survives any mishap.

Forms that sort themselves

We turn the forms and inquiries you receive into clean records ready for follow-up. No one loses a contact for lack of time to enter it.

Content, products, and recommendations

Content that finds its audience

We tag your content and your users, and recommend to each one what interests them most. Usage goes up and drop-off goes down.

Catalogs that converse

We describe and tag your products or services so each customer sees first what suits them best.

Living directories

We keep a public directory always up to date from your database, with photos, credentials, and smart ordering. Nothing has to be edited by hand.

People, knowledge, and trust

Verifiable credentials

We automate the recording of certifications, diplomas, and licenses, with full traceability. Your titles and endorsements build trust.

Evaluation with judgment

We create agents that review work or processes against your own criteria and leave the final decision to a person. Faster, without losing human judgment.

Manuals with your experience

We turn what your key people know into manuals, guides, and courses with professional structure. Your people's know-how becomes available to everyone.

Operations and control

Access and memberships on autopilot

We connect billing, sign-up, and permissions so each person gets into what they paid for, with no manual tasks. Your team stops entering sign-ups by hand.

Campaigns at a human pace

We segment, personalize, and space out your mailings so they arrive the way a person would send them. We protect your email reputation and your relationship with customers.

Routines with human oversight

We schedule recurring tasks that prepare and propose, and ask for approval before acting when something cannot be undone. They save time without taking control from anyone.

Health dashboard

Every week we check that everything works: site, backups, access, and routines. We alert you before something fails.
