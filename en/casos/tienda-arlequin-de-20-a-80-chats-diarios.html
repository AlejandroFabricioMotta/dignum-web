Success story

Tienda Arlequín, from 20 to 80 daily chats

October 29, 2026 · Gift shop

Case from our partner NeuralActions, a solution we represent internationally.

Fact sheet

ClientTienda Arlequín (Catamarca, Argentina)
IndustryGift shop
SolutionAutomatic WhatsApp messages to handle inquiries and improve conversions
Reported results+42% conversions in 30 days; +65% automatic 24/7 replies; −30% in average response time; from 20 to more than 80 daily chats

The challenge. A small store handles its inquiries with few hands. When messages exceed what the team can answer, the customer goes elsewhere.

What was done. NeuralActions set up automatic WhatsApp messages that answer frequent questions and accompany the customer through to purchase, at any hour.

Results, according to NeuralActions. In 30 days conversions rose 42%, automatic 24/7 replies grew 65%, and response time fell 30%. The store went from handling 20 chats a day to more than 80, without adding staff.

The question still to answer. Handling four times as many conversations with the same team is an achievement, but also a signal to look at closely.

Something to think about. If the volume of inquiries at your business multiplied by four, what would you have to watch so your team doesn't pay the price?

Source: CX NeuralActions, accessed on October 29, 2026.
