Success story

A directory that updates from its database

These three cases correspond to projects of the Global Mentors Network, an organization close to Dignum and independent of it.

The challenge. A public directory of certified mentors that had to always reflect the official information, in three languages, with no manual work.

What we did. We connected the directory to the official database. Every week, data, photos, and credentials are updated, and the cards are ordered by defined criteria. With a prior copy of each file and a review sample for bulk changes.

What changed. An always up-to-date directory, with no manual editing, with traceability of every change.

How we care for people. We publish only the data each person agreed to show and we keep previous versions.
