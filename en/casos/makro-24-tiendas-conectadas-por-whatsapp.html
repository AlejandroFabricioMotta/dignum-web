Success story

Makro, 24 stores connected through WhatsApp

October 29, 2026 · Retail

Case from our partner NeuralActions, a solution we represent internationally.

Fact sheet

ClientMakro Argentina (network of 24 stores)
IndustryRetail
SolutionInteractive QR codes in the showroom and store that open a WhatsApp conversation
Reported results24 branches connected at once; 16,500 monthly interactions; 24/7 service

The challenge. A network of 24 stores has thousands of customers on the floor at the same time, each with a different question. In-person service can't reach everyone, and what exists doesn't extend beyond store hours.

What was done. NeuralActions digitized the showroom and store with interactive QR codes across the network. Customers scan and chat on WhatsApp with an assistant connected to each branch, with service 24 hours a day.

Results, according to NeuralActions. All 24 branches work connected at the same time and the network logs about 16,500 interactions per month.

Something to think about. If each branch connected its customers with an assistant, what would your teams do with the time they now spend answering the same thing a hundred times?

Source: CX NeuralActions, accessed on October 29, 2026.
